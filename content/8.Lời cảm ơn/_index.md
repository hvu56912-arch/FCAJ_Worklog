## A Message of Gratitude to the Mentors and Technology Team in My AWS Internship Environment in Vietnam

**Dear Mentors, members of the technology team, and everyone who accompanied and supported me throughout my internship,**

First and foremost, I would like to express my sincere and heartfelt gratitude to everyone who took the time to guide me, share their knowledge, and give me the opportunity to learn and gain experience in a professional, friendly, and supportive working environment.

For me, this internship was not only an opportunity to explore new knowledge about **Amazon Web Services (AWS)** but also a journey that helped me grow in the way I think, work, and approach my future career. From my first days of feeling unfamiliar with cloud computing services, I gradually learned to research documentation independently, ask questions, gain hands-on experience, and verify my own results.

I am especially grateful to the Mentors and technical team for patiently answering my questions and sharing their practical experience. They helped me understand that, in technology, what matters is not simply making a system work, but also understanding **why a particular solution is chosen, how the system operates, and how to ensure its security and efficiency**.

While researching and working on the project **“Design and Deployment of a Secure Web Server on AWS Using VPC, EC2, S3, and CloudWatch”**, I was able to connect the theoretical knowledge I had learned at university with practical applications in network architecture, access management, data security, server deployment, and system monitoring. The feedback, guidance, and encouragement I received were invaluable sources of motivation that inspired me to keep improving myself.

I would also like to thank my fellow interns for exchanging ideas, sharing learning materials, and supporting one another throughout our studies, hands-on practice, and workshop development. With their companionship, challenges became opportunities for us to learn and grow together.

Beyond the technical knowledge I gained, I was genuinely impressed by the **workspace, surroundings, and welcoming culture** of my internship environment. From the technology team to the support staff, security personnel, and everyone who quietly contributes to maintaining the workplace each day, I always felt welcomed, comfortable, and supported. Their kindness and dedication made this place feel like **a second family** to me.

Although my internship lasted only a limited time, the knowledge, experiences, and kindness I received will remain a meaningful part of my journey as I continue my studies and build my career. I know that I still have much to learn, and I will keep striving to live up to the guidance and trust everyone has placed in me.

Once again, **I would like to express my deepest gratitude to the Mentors, the entire technology team, and everyone who accompanied and supported me during my AWS internship in Vietnam**.

I wish you all good health, happiness, lasting passion for your work, and continued success. May the team continue to grow and remain an inspiring environment that nurtures and empowers many future generations of interns.

**Thank you sincerely!**

*With gratitude and respect,*
